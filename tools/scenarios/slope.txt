# Slopes inside Steinvik (4): the paths from the square to the buildings and the streets go up banks
# by ramps, not steps (no step over 0.6 m per 1.5 m off the pads), and the pads keep their level
# floors. Settlement 10 in slope-10.txt. Run twice: the second run must find little or nothing left
# to cut (log lines "a ramp of N spots", "spots of its streets"). About 5 min: LF_TIMEOUT=600.
god
env 0.4 Clear
goto 4
waitready 240
wait 30
waitready 240
pathcheck 4
reject BANK :: pathcheck 4
expect worst deviation 0.0 :: padcheck 4
roadcheck 4
doorcheck 4
reject BLOCKED :: doorcheck 4
mat
seams 200
quit

# What workers carry home (0.7.0): a farmer a small crate on the back (it was the whole Turnip plant
# with its leaves, which looked like a bush in the air behind the head), a woodcutter a log across the
# shoulder blades, a stonecutter a stone, each flat against the back and moving with it, not floating.
# Settlement 4 (Steinvik); the game runs at speed 3 so that loads come by sooner. The dump lines give
# the parts of the item models the loads are copied from.
god
env 0.4 Clear
dump CargoCrate
dump RoundLog
dump Stone
goto 4
waitready 400
hidehud on
hideme
speed 3
until watching :: watch Farmer]+carrying
wait 0.3
shot cargo_farmer
until watching :: watch Woodcutter]+carrying
wait 0.3
shot cargo_woodcutter
until watching :: watch Stonecutter]+carrying
wait 0.3
shot cargo_stonecutter
speed 1
cam
quit

# About 6 min: LF_TIMEOUT=600.
# The road: found over dry land, painted, cleared, lit. Run twice: the second run must show the
# same road at once, without waiting for it to be made again.
god
env 0.4 Clear
goto 1
roads
waitready 180
roadcheck 1
expect walked :: roadcheck 1
reject BLOCKED :: roadcheck 1
reject LAMP INSIDE :: roadcheck 1
roadtp 1 dry 150
wait 20
hidehud on
roadview 1 dry
shot road_at_once
wait 130
roads
seams 200
shot road_day
lampview
shot road_lamp
env 0.97 Clear
wait 6
roadview 1 dry
shot road_night
env 0.4 Clear
cam
hidehud off
quit

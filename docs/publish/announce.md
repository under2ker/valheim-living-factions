## Discord, English

```
Living Factions 0.8.1

What is new:
- Roads choose gentler routes, follow the ground evenly and keep clear of buildings. Settlement ground slopes into building pads
- Terrain work, road paint, clearing and lamps respect player-built pieces and player-worked ground
- Hired fighters remember their employer, follow only that player through portals and judge hostility by that player's standing
- A failed hire costs nothing, and dead fighters no longer consume the hire limit
- Drag the chronicle anywhere or collapse it to its heading and day. Both choices are saved
- The dialogue explains the latest changes of standing
- Special wares are paginated, and an invalid trade.cfg row no longer hides later offers
- Night-watch challenges and theft alarms target the involved player. A denied chest open starts no theft countdown
- Player actions, commands, gifts and trades are validated more strictly. Faction saves are promoted only after a complete write
- All 0.8.0 fixes are included: wars in either faction-id order, deterministic relations, owner-based hired fighters and tamed animals, and malformed guest message checks

Get it on Thunderstore (r2modman) or GitHub: https://github.com/under2ker/valheim-living-factions/releases
Everyone needs 0.8.x, the server too. 0.7.x does not play with 0.8.x. Worlds from 0.7.0 and 0.8.0 carry over. Back up your world before updating.
```

## Discord, русский

```
Living Factions 0.6.5

Что нового:
- Мечи фракций, семь штук. Шесть продают торговцы своих фракций, корневую дубину грейдворфов забирают у их стражей

Скачать: Thunderstore (через r2modman) или GitHub: https://github.com/under2ker/valheim-living-factions/releases
```

## Discord, English

```
Living Factions 0.6.5

What is new:
- Seven faction swords. Six are sold by the traders of their faction; the Greydwarf root club is taken from their guards
- lf rewind takes the factions back to an earlier day and leaves the world as it is. lf advance keeps a copy first, so a jump too far can be undone
- Settlements check themselves: buildings missing after a restored world backup are put up again, the ground under them levelled
- Fixed: a guest with other HUD mods saw no chronicle under the minimap
- Fixed: the mod's items were named in English in games in other languages
- Fixed: a trader with eight offers did not show the last one

Get it on Thunderstore (r2modman) or GitHub: https://github.com/under2ker/valheim-living-factions/releases
Everyone needs the mod, the server too. Any 0.6.x works with any 0.6.x.
```

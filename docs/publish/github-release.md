### ⚔️ War on Foot

- **Bands on the move:** a settlement short of wood, stone, food or metal sends two to six people up to 1.4 km to where it is plentiful. They carry food, may lose men on the way and bring the load home.
- **Raids march:** the band walks to its target, fights at the gate and carries its loot home. Near you, bands are real people walking the land.
- **War campaigns:** scouts, then a war camp of tents, stakes and banners more than halfway there, then the assault. A supply line from home, allies of both sides joining, counterattacks a day after a place falls. Arms cost metal, and long campaigns wear a faction out. A campaign calls itself off when its target changes hands, and a war camp taken by the enemy becomes a plain camp of theirs.
- **Outposts:** a small camp where the land gives what home lacks, or a watch post halfway along a long road.

### 🏘️ Living Settlements

- **Night:** everyone sleeps in the beds of their own house, two to a bed, and lies still.
- **A thief in the night:** open their chest after dark and a count starts. Take something of theirs and stay too long, and the village wakes. Your own things put in a chest stay yours.
- **Carriers fill the chests:** wood, stone, carrots, turnips, barley and meat really go into the storehouse.
- **Every clan builds its own way:** Raven dark shingles and carved ravens, Wolf log walls and hipped thatch, Stag porches. Fulings and Greydwarfs live in homes of their own; Fuling workshops have hide roofs. Cities raise stone walls with towers.

### 🤝 You and the Factions

- **Envoys:** every settlement has an envoy's tent by its road out. The envoy talks to you whatever the faction thinks of you, even at war: gifts, tribute, a plea for peace, and plain words about what stands between you.
- **Blows by standing:** they fight back only after a few blows, more the better they like you, and warn you before a friendship breaks. No more "hit one, anger all".
- **Hired fighters by standing:** two for 10 minutes, four for 15 from friends, six for 24 from allies. They go through portals with you.
- **Seven faction shields** (+20% armour and block), and the clans' fighters carry their shield and sword.
- **Their property is theirs:** crops, hives and building pieces can no longer be taken for free.

### 🗺️ Map and Screen

- Settlement names on the large map open up into their state; villagers near you are dots on the minimap. Alt+H hides the chronicle.

### 🛠️ Fixes

- **Hoe levelling:** levelling a large area no longer gets ditches and humps along the borders of terrain tiles. Ditches dug before stay: level them over once.
- **Roads:** no more steps on slopes, no roads into a wall, rocks and bushes cleared, caravans no longer stuck on the road.
- **Villages on slopes:** a building's level ground is fitted to its neighbours, and ramps run along the paths from the edge of each pad (older settlements are fitted once).
- **Shields** sit like the game's own, with no arm through them.
- **False theft** for a guest walking into a village in multiplayer.
- **Reputation fade** is a setting now (World/ReputationDecay, 0 = never).
- **Saves:** a faction state file that cannot be read no longer starts the factions over: the backup beside it or the latest dated copy is used. A state from a newer version of the mod is not loaded.
- **Dialogue:** the way out is always on screen, even when a friendly faction offers more than fits.
- **Multiplayer:** `lf rewind` on the server resets what each guest had built in the timeline taken back.

### 🧪 Tested In-Game

1. Own goods put into a chest and taken out again, by day and at night: no theft, no alarm. The household's coins taken at night: theft and the alarm.
2. Carriers put their loads into the storehouse chests.
3. 16 shield carriers in three clans measured: no forearm through any board. 24 sleepers tracked: none moved.
4. The roads into two settlements walked: nothing in the way, gates on the road.
5. Campaigns whose target changed hands, was already theirs, or whose scouts fell: each one ended, no band marched. A war camp taken: its tents and stakes came down (28 pieces to 11).
6. The faction state file broken (with and without a backup) and from a newer version: the factions came back each time. With the file missing they started anew, as they should.
7. On a dedicated server with a guest: a guest building a village, and a rewind on the server.

### ⚠️ Important

Everyone needs version 0.7.x, the server too: 0.6.x and 0.7.x do not play together. On the first visit each settlement puts up its houses, square, camps, walls and lamps again once: give it a minute. Back up your world before updating.

## Short description

```
Seven NPC factions build villages piece by piece, fell and replant the forest, trade and go to war on their own. Trade, hire fighters, take boss hunts, earn a relic axe and a sword from every faction. Single-player and servers. EN, RU, PL, DE, FR, ES, PT.
```

## Description

```
Living Factions brings seven NPC factions into your Valheim world. They set up camps, gather wood, stone and food, put up buildings one piece at a time and grow into villages, towns and cities. They trade, fall out, go to war and take each other's settlements, whether you are around or not. And you can take part.

THE FACTIONS
- Raven Clan: humans of the Meadows and Black Forest. Even-tempered, keep their word.
- Wolf Clan: humans of the Black Forest and Mountains. Quick to fight.
- Stag Clan: humans of the Meadows and Plains. Traders, peaceful.
- Fuling Horde of the Plains. Raiders.
- Dvergr Hold of the Mistlands. Traders, proud.
- Greydwarf Brood of the Black Forest. Spread fast, do not trade.
- Draugr Court of the Swamp. Hate the living.

A WORLD THAT KEEPS GOING
- Real buildings made of the game's own pieces, put up in order.
- People have a day: woodcutters, stonecutters, farmers, carriers, visits, sleep, guards on the round and on the watchtower.
- Woodcutters fell real trees away from the houses and plant saplings in their place.
- Roads with lamp posts between settlements, caravans with guards.
- Borders, trade, incidents, wars, raids and captured settlements. If you are in a village when it is raided, the raiders really come.
- Faction lands on the map in each faction's colour, and a chronicle under the minimap.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- Talk to anyone, give gifts, trade, buy relics, hire two fighters for a while.
- Make an alliance at standing 70, declare war or pay for peace.
- Take boss hunts for bounties.
- Earn seven relic axes and seven swords, one of each per faction.

PLAYING TOGETHER
Single-player, a player hosting, and dedicated servers. Everyone needs the mod, the server too (any 0.6.x works with any 0.6.x).

BACKUPS
Restoring a world backup takes the factions back too. "lf rewind" in the console takes the factions back without touching the world.

Needs BepInExPack Valheim and Jotunn. Back up your world before the first run: settlements level the ground and place pieces.

Feedback and bug reports: https://discord.gg/qVfAq3fqj6
Source and releases: https://github.com/under2ker/valheim-living-factions

Made with the help of AI (Claude Code). No game files, decompiled code or extracted assets are included.
```

## Changelog 0.6.5

```
- Seven faction swords: six sold by the traders of their faction, the Greydwarf root club taken from their guards.
- lf rewind: takes the factions back to an earlier day while the world stays as it is. lf advance keeps a copy first, so a jump too far can be undone.
- Settlements check themselves: buildings missing after a restored backup are put up again, the ground under them levelled.
- Fixed: a guest with other HUD mods saw no chronicle under the minimap.
- Fixed: the mod's items were named in English in games in other languages until a restart.
- Fixed: a trader with more than seven offers hid the last ones.
```
